# Traffic Sensor Data Capture Instructions

## 1. Preparation
*   **QR Code**: Stick the QR code **directly below (or above)** the traffic sensor casing.
*   **Distance**: Keep the gap between the sensor and QR code consistent (e.g., 2-5 cm).

---

## 2. The "Golden Set" (Calibration)
*Goal: Perfect, clear data to know exactly what the sensors look like.*

### 📸 Photos (15-20 total)
**Instructions**: Hold phone straight and steady. Ensure good lighting.
*   **Single Sensor**: Take ~20 photos of just **one** sensor + QR code visible on the image (Red light, Yellow light, Off and Green if applicable).
*   **Multiple Sensors**: Take ~10 photos with **several** sensors and QR codes visible on the image.

### 🎥 Videos (4-6 total)
**Instructions**: Keep holding phone straight and steady. Short duration (~5-10 seconds max).
*   **Single Sensor**: Record ~3 videos with just one sensor visible at the same time.
*   **Multiple Sensors**: Record ~3 videos with several sensors visible at the same time.

---

## 3. Natural Usage (Real-World Testing)
*Goal: "Messy" data to make the detection robust against mistakes and different conditions.*

### 📸 Photos (Variety)
Take photos as if you were working normally, this could be:
*   **Angles**: Slight left/right, looking up/down.
*   **Distance**: Some close-up, some from 2-3 meters away.
*   **Lighting**: Shadows, bright sun, or indoor dim light.

### 🎥 Videos (Movement)
Record videos moving as a user would naturally, this could be:
*   **Scanning**: Moving the camera from one sensor to another.
*   **Approaching**: Walking in towards the sensor while recording.
*   **Handheld**: Shaking the camera a little.

---

## 4. Exporting
1.  Go to the **Manage Captured Data** screen in the app.
2.  Tap the **Download** button (top right icon).
3.  Send the `.zip` file.
